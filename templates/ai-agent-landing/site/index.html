How it works

From page to handoff before you would have found your glasses.

Duskline takes first response on the services you choose, during the hours you choose. It works an incident the way your team would: evidence first, smallest safe action second.

  1. 01 / 03:12:04

    It takes the page

    Point any alert source at Duskline for the services and hours you pick. When something fires it acknowledges within seconds, so the escalation timer only reaches a person when it should.

  2. 02 / 03:12:09

    It gathers evidence

    Before touching anything, it reads. Traces, logs, recent deploys, the runbook for that alert, and what happened the last time it fired.

    • traces · 15 min
    • deploys · last 3
    • runbook · checkout-latency
    • last seen · 28 Sep

  3. 03 / 03:13:02

    It takes the smallest safe action

    If your policy allows the fix, Duskline makes it and watches the graphs to confirm it worked. If the fix needs permission, it asks the right person with everything they need to answer in one tap.

    rollback_deploy → #4820

    allowed by policy · verified 90 s

  4. 04 / 03:15:10

    It writes the handoff

    You wake up to four lines: what broke, why, what changed, and what still needs a human. The full trail is one click away, and a postmortem draft is waiting if you want it.

Capabilities

A colleague for the night, not a chatbot with a pager.

Reads the runbook before it touches anything

Duskline follows the procedures your team already wrote and tells you which step it is on. Where a runbook is missing, it says so instead of improvising.

runbooks/checkout-latency.md

1. Check the error rate

2. Compare against last deploy

3. Regressed after a deploy? Roll back

4. Otherwise scale read replicas

5. Still slow? Wake the owner

Guardrails

You draw the line. It stays behind it.

Nothing happens at night that you did not agree to in daylight. One short policy file per service decides what Duskline may do alone, what needs a yes, and what is off the table.

Allowed
Reversible actions it may take alone, like restarting a worker or rolling back a recent deploy.
Ask first
Actions that need a named person to say yes. One tap from a phone, with the evidence attached.
Never
Things it will not do, whoever asks and however urgent the page sounds.
