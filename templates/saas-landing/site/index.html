FAQ

Questions, answered

Not here? Write to hello@brindle.example and a person will reply within a working day.

Who counts as an approver?

Anyone who can approve or decline a request in a given month. People who only submit requests are always free, however many there are.

Does Brindle replace our accounting system?

No. Brindle sits in front of it. Requests are approved here, then arrive in your ledger as coded purchase orders, ready to match against the invoice.

How long does it take to set up?

Most teams send their first real request the same afternoon. Import your people, write two or three routing rules, and connect chat. Budgets and accounting sync can follow later.

What happens when an approver is away?

They name a stand-in for the dates they are out, or Brindle reads it from their calendar. Requests go to the stand-in automatically and the trail records who approved on whose behalf.

Can we change plan or cancel at any time?

Yes. Move up or down whenever you like and we adjust the next bill. If you cancel a yearly plan we refund the months you have not used.

How is our data protected?

Everything is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is logged, and you choose the region your data lives in. Our security overview and latest independent audit report are available on request.
