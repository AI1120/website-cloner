The problem

Your building has three bins. None of them is for food.

Houses get a green cart at the kerb. Apartments get a chute. So the peels, grounds and leftovers of a few hundred people go straight to landfill, every single week.

How it works

Three steps. You only do the middle one.

No sorting quiz, no smell, no fruit flies. If it grew, it goes.

  1. 01

    We set up every floor

    Sealed, pedal-operated bins sized for your building. Installation takes one morning and needs no plumbing or power.

  2. 02

    You toss your scraps

    Whenever you like, a few steps from your door. Our electric trikes swap the liners every Thursday before 9 a.m.

  3. 03

    Soil comes back

    Each spring your building gets its share of finished compost for planters, the roof garden, or the tree pit out front.

Made for both sides of the lobby door.

Our story

It started in a bin room.

We met taking out the trash. Same building, same floor, same guilty look at a bag that was mostly vegetable peelings.

Noor had spent six years planning waste routes for the city. Tomasz built cargo trikes. Between us we knew that the hard part of composting is not the composting. It is getting a clean bin within twenty steps of every kitchen, and emptying it before anyone has a reason to complain.

So that is the whole company. Bins close by, emptied on time, and something good coming back. If that sounds like your building, we would love to hear from you.
